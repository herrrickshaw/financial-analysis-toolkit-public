# India state/UT government investment-incentive websites — full reference

Every state's/UT's official industries department, investment-promotion agency (IPA), and industrial land
portal, as actually used to source `docs/INDIA_STATE_INVESTMENT_INCENTIVES.md` and
`docs/INDIA_INDUSTRIAL_LAND_COST_BENCHMARKS.md`. This is the "which government website has this state's
numbers" index — the two docs above have the actual rates and citations; this one is just the map of where
to look, state by state, for the full set of 36 states/UTs.

**Status column**: "Confirmed" means this session directly researched and cited that URL. "Pending" means
the state is queued in an active research pass (results not yet in) — for those rows, no URL is listed yet
rather than guessing one. Do not treat a "Confirmed" URL as guaranteeing a usable *rate* was found there —
several confirmed portals yielded "not confirmed in source" for specific numbers; see the two source docs.

## States/UTs in progress (18) — Pending

A research pass is actively running for all 18 remaining states/UTs as of this writing. No URL is listed
below yet — this section will be filled in with confirmed portals and rates once that research returns,
following the exact same discipline as above (confirmed sources only, "not confirmed in source" where a
portal yields nothing).

**North-East (7)**, expected to share the central UNNATI 2024 overlay confirmed for Assam above: Arunachal
Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura.

**Hill/central states (5)**: Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Goa.

**Union Territories (6)**: Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Puducherry, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu,
Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep. (J&K and Ladakh are a priority within this batch: J&K historically
had a special, more generous central industrial package — the "New Industrial Development Scheme for J&K"
2017 — whose current status, post the 2019 UT reorganization, needs confirming, analogous to how NEIDS 2017
for the North-East expired and was replaced by UNNATI 2024.)
